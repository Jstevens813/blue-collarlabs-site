PLA+ — the tougher everyday filament

PLA+ prints as easy as regular PLA but with noticeably better impact strength and layer adhesion — our pick when a part needs to take a knock, not just sit on a desk. eSUN's PLA+ is the shop standard.

PETG & PETG-CF — functional parts

PETG HF is our go-to for durable, weather-resistant parts that print nearly as fast as PLA. When a part needs real stiffness, PETG-CF adds carbon fiber for strength and a finish that hides layer lines — just run it with a hardened steel nozzle, because carbon fiber eats brass. Bambu sells PETG-CF direct, so that link goes to their store.
