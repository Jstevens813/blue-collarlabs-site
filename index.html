Why Shop With Blue-Collar Labs?

Most desk toys are injection-molded overseas and sold by the container. Ours are different. Each design is printed in small batches so we can check every layer line, test every moving part and fix anything that does not meet our standard before it leaves the shop. We print in tough PLA and PLA+ filament for crisp detail and everyday durability, and we stand behind what we make: if a product ever arrives with a genuine manufacturing defect, reach out with a photo and we will make it right. Because we are a small Florida shop and not a faceless marketplace seller, you also get fast, honest communication — send us a message and a real person from the shop answers.

How Every Order Is Made

Every Blue-Collar Labs product follows the same path. First we model and prototype the design, printing test versions until the mechanism works the way it should — triggers click, launchers launch and keychains survive a pocket full of keys. Then we print production runs on our Bambu Lab P1S printers, the same machines we recommend on our gear page if you want to start printing yourself.

Finished parts are cleaned, assembled, function-tested and photographed in our own workshop — the print station you see here is the real bench where your order was made. Finally we pack it with care and ship it from Florida with tracking on every order.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are your products really 3D printed?

Yes. Every desk toy, launcher, crossbow and keychain is designed and 3D printed in our own small shop in St. Petersburg, Florida, then assembled, tested and inspected by hand before it ships.

How fast do orders ship?

Orders are printed, checked and shipped from Florida as quickly as possible, and every order is completed securely through Etsy with tracking and buyer protection.

What are the toys made of?

Most products are printed in tough PLA and PLA+ filament on Bambu Lab printers, chosen for clean detail, bright colors and everyday durability at a desk, in a workshop or in a truck cab.

Do you take custom 3D printing requests?

We love hearing ideas. Message us through Etsy or Facebook with what you are dreaming up and we will tell you honestly whether we can design and print it.
